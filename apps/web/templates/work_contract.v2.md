## 7. Site access and preparation
The Client will give safe access to the work area, working power and water, and will remove furniture and personal belongings unless the scope of work says otherwise.

## 8. Hidden conditions
If concealed conditions are found, such as moisture, subfloor damage or an uneven surface, the Contractor will tell the Client before continuing. Any extra work needed is handled as a change order.

## 9. Change orders
Any change to the scope of work, price or schedule must be agreed in writing by both parties before the work is done.

## 10. Warranty
The Contractor warrants its workmanship for one (1) year from completion. The warranty does not cover damage from water, moisture, misuse, heavy impact, pre-existing site conditions or normal wear and natural movement of materials.

## 11. Termination
Either party may end this contract by written notice if the other party materially breaches it and does not fix the breach within 7 days of written notice. The Client will pay for work completed and for materials already ordered, including special-order materials that cannot be returned.

## 12. General
This contract is the entire agreement between the parties and replaces earlier discussions. It is governed by the laws of the Province of Alberta and the federal laws of Canada that apply there.
